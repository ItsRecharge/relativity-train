🌀 Equivalence principle (the GR bonus)

The train is now accelerating. Accelerated frames are where special relativity hands over to general relativity: inside, acceleration is indistinguishable from a uniform gravitational field pointing backwards.

Clocks at the front of the train run faster than at the rear — exactly like clocks higher in a gravity well:

ratefront / raterear ≈ 1 + aL/c²

🚄 Relativity Express — field guide

Everything on screen is computed live from the Lorentz transform — no canned animations. One button swaps which frame is "at rest," and every effect flips with it.

The two frames

  • Platform frame — Bob at rest. The train is length-contracted; Alice's light clock zigzags and ticks slow.
  • Train frame — Alice at rest. Now the world contracts and streams past; Bob's clock is the slow one. Both are right.

Thought experiments

  • ⚡ Lightning — the 1916 original. Simultaneous strikes in one frame are not simultaneous in the other. Run it in both frames.
  • 🚇 Tunnel paradox — an 80 m train "fits" a 60 m tunnel… only in the tunnel's frame. The resolution is on the doors.
  • ⏱ Light-clock race — both clocks freshly zeroed; count ticks until the home clock hits 12.
  • 🌀 Accelerating train — the general-relativity doorway: acceleration ≡ gravity, front clock outruns the rear.

Relativistic optics (train view)

  • Doppler — scenery blueshifts ahead, redshifts behind: D = 1/γ(1−β·cosθ).
  • Aberration — the view crowds toward your direction of motion.
  • Headlight — forward light beams brighter ∝ D².
  • Terrell rotation (platform view) — cameras don't photograph contraction; light-travel delays make the train look rotated. Toggle it and orbit around.
